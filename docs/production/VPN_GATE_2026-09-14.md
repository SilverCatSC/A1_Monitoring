## Required policy declaration

The functional requirement is simultaneous access, but it does not by itself
select a split-routing policy. Before this gate can close, the owner must record
one intended path for each service — `VPN exit` or `direct physical connection`
— including required subdomains and both IP families. The earlier observation of
direct-routing exceptions for Auto.ru and Avito is historical evidence, not a
current policy declaration or a substitute for a readable current rule.

## Local admission boundary

Once the owner has a real policy declaration and browser evidence, a full
MacBook host cycle additionally requires a fresh, owner-authored local record.
Its exact 0700/0600 storage, schema, 24-hour limit and fail-closed behavior are
defined in the [VPN admission contract](VPN_ADMISSION_CONTRACT_2026-09-14.md).
That record does not fill the unresolved egress-evidence gap documented here,
and it never changes, reconnects or exports VPSUS.

## Assessment and limitation

The current Mac now has tunnel-connected, domain-reachability and ordinary
browser functional-access evidence. Sampled IPv4 routes for all three services
currently enter `utun4`; IPv6 attribution is unresolved. This does **not** prove
that Auto.ru and Avito leave directly, because their browser egress rule inside
the live PacketTunnel cannot be read. The historical direct-rule observation is
useful, but not a substitute after the active binary configuration changed.

To close the split-routing fact, record the intended policy, make the VPSUS rule
screen readable and capture it read-only, then perform one controlled
browser-level check for ChatGPT, Auto.ru and Avito without CAPTCHA bypass or a
VPN reconnect. The rule and the proof must cover IPv4 and IPv6. CAPTCHA, 401,
403, 429, timeout or a changed page remain technical results, never a reason to
weaken the VPN or bypass platform protection.

This record closes neither the direct-browser routing check nor M7. The current
MacBook scope still requires the controlled shadow-run outcome and owner
acceptance; if owner enables a schedule, it also requires a separate per-user
LaunchAgent trigger gate. MSI/Windows repetition is a fallback-only procedure,
not a current MacBook release requirement.
